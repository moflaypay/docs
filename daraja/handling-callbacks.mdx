---

Daraja sends payment callbacks after Safaricom processes an M-Pesa request. When you use Moflay, Moflay receives that callback, updates the payment and transaction state, then sends signed Moflay webhooks to your backend.

## What Moflay adds

Moflay adds a production event layer around Daraja callbacks:

- Receiving the Safaricom callback
- Matching the callback to the payment attempt
- Updating the payment and transaction status
- Sending Moflay webhook events to your configured endpoint

## What your backend should handle

Your backend should consume Moflay webhooks instead of raw Daraja callbacks.

Use webhooks to:

- Mark orders as paid
- Handle failed payments
- Unlock access after payment
- Keep internal transaction state in sync

## Safaricom callbacks vs Moflay webhooks

| Term | Sender | Receiver | Your responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Safaricom callback | Safaricom Daraja | Moflay | Moflay receives and processes it |
| Moflay webhook | Moflay | Your backend | Verify and process the event |
